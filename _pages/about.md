# Research and project experience

**Beyond Static Grounding: Behavioral Contracts for Trustworthy Retrieval-Augmented Generation** (EMNLP 2026 Main, equal contribution co-first author), 2025 - 2026
Conventional RAG evaluation measures answer quality under fixed evidence, and therefore cannot reveal how a system should behave when evidence changes. This work introduces Dynamic Evidence Contracts, an application-dependent pattern that specifies expected behavior under four evidence interventions (deletion, conflict, distraction, and permutation), together with the Intervention Compliance Score (ICS) to summarize compliance and stability. Across five datasets, we show that evaluated open-source Qwen3-8B RAG systems exhibit passive NLI-detected refutation but limited explicit conflict handling, and we present DOVE-RAG, a reference instantiation whose explicit evidence-specific behavior is supported by fixed-footer controls and a blinded human study.

**Knowledge Distillation Model Placement Optimization** (CCF-C, first author), Oct. 2024 - May 2025
Aimed at the insufficient computing power of edge devices, this work proposes distilling a teacher model into a student model and then placing the student model within the network. The proposed algorithm significantly reduces model inference cost and user access bandwidth overhead, decreasing the total overhead by 12.8% on average.

**Starfish-inspired Ultra-high Performance Flexible Piezoresistive Sensor and Its Intelligent Health Monitoring and Smart Production System** (National College Students' Innovation Training Program), Apr. 2024 - May 2025
I collected a dataset mapping piezoresistive sensor readings to joint bending angles, trained a detection model using a random forest algorithm, and deployed the corresponding backend to enable real-time monitoring of Parkinson's patients' joint bending and health status. Completed with an Excellent rating.
