Research and Project Experience
Knowledge Distillation Model Placement Optimization — First author, Oct. 2024 – May 2025
Aimed at the insufficient computing power of edge devices, this work proposes distilling a teacher model into a student model and then placing the student model within the network. The proposed algorithm significantly reduces both model inference cost and user access bandwidth overhead, decreasing the total overhead by 12.8% on average.
Direct Prediction of Dynamically Missing Spatiotemporal Data Graphs — Second author, under review at TKDE (CCF-A, IF 10.4), Aug. 2025 – Jan. 2026
This work focuses on the dynamic missingness that is pervasive in real-world spatiotemporal data, which causes a marked performance drop for existing forecasting methods that fail to account for such missingness. We design an efficient missingness-aware neural architecture together with an improved training strategy, reconstructing an explicit missingness-aware self-attention mechanism that lowers the computational cost of multi-stage training, and validate the approach on multiple datasets.
Starfish-inspired Ultra-high Performance Flexible Piezoresistive Sensor and Its Intelligent Health Monitoring and Smart Production System — National College Students' Innovation Training Program, Apr. 2024 – May 2025
I collected a dataset mapping piezoresistive sensor readings to joint bending angles, trained a detection model using a random forest algorithm, and deployed the corresponding backend to enable real-time monitoring of Parkinson's patients' joint bending and health status. Completed with an Excellent rating.
